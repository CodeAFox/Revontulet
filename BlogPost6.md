# Slime Hearder

Slime Hearder is the name of the final game that I have designed. It’s a cozy, two-level game, where the player has to heard slimes into chests to advance to the next level. 
It is a 2d game with the aesthetics leaning more into pixel games, where the background music also reflects that being a 2-bit song.

The first level is a simple one, with three basic slimes that move about the map randomly, only escaping if the player gets too close. The slimes do not avoid the chests, allowing for the player to just look around and enjoy the music if they choose and the slimes might just solve the puzzle by themselves.

The second level is a bit more complicated with the addition of a new slime variant as well as objects to push around and potentially help trap the new and faster slimes.
The new slime variant is not only faster, but also smarter; they move around at the same speed as the player, cleverly avoiding the chests making it harder for the player to trap them. This is where the new pushable objects come in however; they are heavy, even for the player it takes a bit of strength to push around, but for the slimes? They can barely push it. This makes it possible for the player to set up traps, into which they can lure the speedy slimes and force them to go into the chests with nowhere else to escape to.

Lastly, the game also includes a simple pause menu so stepping away from the game is not impossible. Furthermore, there is also a settings menu that allows for the player to adjust the sounds, including background music and the footsteps of the player and the slimes, in accordance with their needs.

Future expansion could include new slime variants, such as a slime that can escape if the player is not capturing the other slimes fast enough, or a slime that can only go into its designated spot. It could also include objects that the player can pick up and build more complex traps with, allowing for them to strategize and come up with creative solutions to trap the slimes.
In addition to this, it may also be expanded to a two-player mode, so that people can play and strategize together to capture the targets and heard them into the chests.
